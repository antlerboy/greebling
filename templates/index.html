What’s a greeble?

Look closely at a model spaceship. The pipes and boxes stuck to its hull make it look big enough to walk around inside. Model-makers call those details greebles.

I borrowed the word for something I kept noticing. Add a detail to the world and somebody can find a use for it that you never intended.

Benjamin P Taylor
